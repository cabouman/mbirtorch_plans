Flash remediation — Phase 3: the implemented remedies on real scans

The shipped defaults from phase_2d_remedies.html, validated step by step on SiC, Lilly, and the Purdue BGA scan · 2026-07-11/12 · implementation commits a872695 (axial), fcc0e9e (split), 41ecbc2 (lateral warn), dbc9c3b (NSI cleanup) · plan of record: flash_remediation_plan.md (implementation record) · volumes, logs, and figures: /depot/bouman/data/mbirjax_metrics/padding/.

TL;DR — every Phase 2d verdict survived contact with real data.

  • Axial extension (now the default): on SiC it removes the end-slice flash and z-ringing, reconstructs the actual object continuation past the old slab, leaves the interior at the <1% level — and reaches the 0.2% stop at ~iteration 20 where the old shape needed ~49 (≈2.2× faster wall-clock to the default stop). Replicated at the 1024-class.
  • Split extension (taper retired): on Lilly the shipped default meets or beats every Phase 2c yardstick in both regimes — including at a measured worst-case half-slice cut/split mismatch — and the opt-in alignment buys another ~6–12×.
  • Lateral warning: correct on all four scans, and it corrected two of our own labels (z62 is genuinely contained; Lilly is a mild true positive). Following its advice on BGA (lateral 1.5× on top of axial) erases the boundary ring and reveals the board continuing past the old FoV.
  • NSI pipeline cleanup: the automatic geometry now sees the real detector parameters — Lilly ds4 drops from 667 to 572 slices (~14% memory back) with values preserved to 0.62% and the split seam still fixed.

1  Axial extension on SiC (and the axial-only BGA check)

The single-variable A/B: same library, same cached sinogram, same weights, sequence, seed, and regularization — only the slab differs (old forces the pre-extension shape through the set_params escape hatch). SiC is the axial poster case: in-FoV laterally, truncated in rows on one side.

Capsule numbers (v4x, iteration 50): interior (middle-half slab) RMS(new−old)/body = 0.0075; truncated-end 8-slice difference 2.20× body (the removed artifact); untruncated end 0.023. The 1024-class run (v3x_d2x, 1024²×1310) replicates everything, and its old-shape variant agrees with the pre-existing prerelease-era reference recon to rel max diff 7.9×10-7 — the branch changed nothing at the old shape, and the escape-hatch forcing is exact.

Following the warning's advice: lateral 1.5× on top of axial (BGA)

The honest null: on Lilly (the mild 16% one-sided case), lateral 1.25× produced no measurable change at the central slab — annulus means ~10-4 at all shared radii in both variants. The warning correctly describes the data; at ds4 the reconstruction impact is below noise at the probed slices.

4  NSI pipeline cleanup

The NSI flow computed the automatic geometry at model construction, before the real detector pitches and offsets were set — inflating the axial extension by 1/δch (2× at Lilly ds4; ~8× at full resolution) and mis-centering by |det_row_offset|·R/SDD. The fix aligns NSI with the zeiss convention (construct → set_params → auto_set_recon_geometry()) and retires the hand-set recon_slice_offset compensation, with no back-compat shims (a stale entry in an old parameter dict passes through and is overwritten by the auto call). Validated on Lilly:

  • ds4 shape (374, 374, 572) vs the inflated 667 — ~14% of the volume back — decomposing exactly as derived (base 471 + per-end 55/46 from det_row_offset = −3.9 rows), offset +4.50 slices (per-end) vs the old hand +3.90;
  • values preserved: old-flow vs new-flow recons agree on physically z-aligned interior profiles to max rel 0.62% over the whole shared range;
  • the split seam stays fixed under the new flow: ds8 seam 5.6×10-4 (background 2.4×10-5), the formula again choosing hrecon = 9 at a near-worst-case mismatch (−0.45).

5  What this settles, and what remains

  • Settled: all four Phase 2d verdicts are implemented as defaults and validated on real scans (§1 items 1–4 of current_plans.md). Support beats down-weighting, on real data, in every axis tested.
  • Remaining (step E): re-baseline the regression dashboards (+ rebuild the NSI caches with the new builder), the release note, and the annotation + policy-flag record of the regime change.
  • Open questions parked: the z62 RoR-boundary ring in air (now decoupled from truncation); the BGA center-slice spike (a separate, sharply localized artifact — the per-slice noise instrument localizes it to ~one slice); severe-truncation DC removal via an air anchor; the cone scale_recon_shape compensating-slice-growth warning (the p3e extend_axially_to_bound helper is its prototype).
