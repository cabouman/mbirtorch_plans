# Prompt for documenting a session that went badly

Paste this to the Claude that was involved.

---

Something went wrong in this session. Write it up in
`~/claude-notes/claude-behavior/YYYY-MM-DD-short-name.md`.

Be honest and specific. This is a record of your own failure, not a defense of it.

Cover, in this order:

1. **What happened.** The sequence, in order, with what I asked and what you did. Quote me where it matters.
2. **The root failure.** One thing, in one or two sentences. Not a list.
3. **What made it worse.** What you did after the first mistake that cost more time.
4. **The rule that would have prevented it.** State it so a Claude with no memory of this session could follow it.

Then tell me which rule belongs in `~/claude-notes/writing_style.md`, in memory, or in
`CLAUDE.md`, and ask before writing it anywhere.

Follow `~/claude-notes/writing_style.md`. Do not apologize, and do not soften what happened.
